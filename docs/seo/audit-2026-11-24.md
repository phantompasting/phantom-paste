Word counts are visible text after scripts and styles are stripped, so navigation and footer are included. Title and description lengths below are the strings in the HTML. Three blog titles store an apostrophe as `&#x27;`; browsers decode that, and those three fall back under 60 characters once decoded. They are not in the over-60 list.

## Heavy pages

Shared Next.js chunks ship on every URL. Several large chunks returned HTTP 200 with `Content-Length: 0` on HEAD, so a sum of script bytes under-counts the real JavaScript. A production build of this branch reports the real numbers: shared First Load JS is 102 kB (a 45.8 kB chunk plus a 54.2 kB framework chunk). Homepage First Load JS is 112 kB, `/gallery` is 123 kB, `/locations` is 133 kB, and `/blog` is 146 kB. The live crawl only saw the 45.8 kB chunk.

## JSON-LD gaps

What is in good shape: every 200 page emitted JSON-LD. BreadcrumbList is on every crawled page. Blog posts emit Article + FAQPage + Person. Service URLs emit Service. Location pages emit Service plus FAQPage. Homepage emits Organization, WebSite, Service, and FAQPage.

Gaps:

1. **Dead Person node.** `mateoVargasPerson()` sets `url` to `/authors/mateo-vargas` and `image` to `/authors/mateo-vargas-avatar.webp`. Both 404. That Person block is on every blog post, so the author entity Google is asked to trust does not resolve.
2. **No Google Business Profile in `sameAs`.** `lib/business.ts` lists Instagram, LinkedIn, Agency Spotter, X, Yelp, and Pinterest. There is no GBP / Maps URL. This pull request leaves a TODO instead of inventing one. Organization `legalName` on production is still "Phantom Pasting"; this PR sets it to "Phantom Pasting LLC" (not live until a deploy Kelvin approves).
3. **`/work` has no FAQPage** while every case study under it does. `/about` has WebPage + Organization and no FAQPage.
4. **Homepage has no `LocalBusiness` address.** That is intentional in `lib/business.ts` (nationwide service-area business, country-only postal address). Do not "fix" it by adding a city or the entity gets pinned.
5. **Ohio title is an `absolute` metadata title**, so it drops the `| Phantom Pasting` template and still runs long.

## Prioritized fixes

1. Stop pointing Person schema at `/authors/mateo-vargas` and the missing avatar. Either publish a real author URL and image or point `url` at `/blog` and drop the 404 image. Highest leverage because it is on every post.
2. Shorten the eight titles over 60 characters, starting with the two mural posts (96 and 82).
3. Trim the 24 meta descriptions over 160 characters. The fashion-week post (234) and mural post (210) will be cut off in the snippet.
4. Add the Google Business Profile URL to `sameAs` when Kelvin has it. Do not guess.
5. Give `/artists/steven-sued`, `/about`, and `/work` enough unique prose (and a short FAQ on `/work` and `/about`) that they are not the thinnest commercial URLs.
6. Expand tier-2 state templates (Wisconsin through Ohio in the thin list) with real local detail instead of the shared skeleton.
7. Cut the `/blog` index image payload (34 images in the first HTML) and lazy-load below-the-fold gallery cards. `/gallery` HTML is 322 KB.
8. Measure the layout JavaScript with a real transfer-size trace. HEAD `Content-Length` was empty on the big shared chunks, so the current 43 KB figure is not a performance budget.
9. Give `/locations/ohio` a normal title template so the brand is in the SERP title and the string is under 60 characters.
10. Keep quote-within-24-hours copy distinct from install lead time. This PR aligns install lead time to 10–14 business days from final art approval and the proof-of-install report to 7 business days. The live site, until that ships, still says 48 hours / 72 hours / 5–7 day rush in many of those spots.
